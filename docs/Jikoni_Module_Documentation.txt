How Jikoni is organised
Jikoni is Ignis's internal operating system — one place for finance, procurement, inventory, people, partnerships, projects, the raise and governance. The modules are not separate apps; they share one foundation and read from one another.
The full treatment of that foundation — one source of truth, records-with-states, role-based access and segregation of duties, the audit log, and document-driven chains — is in Part A of the Workflows & SOPs guide, and is not repeated here.
How to read this document
The left sidebar lists the modules; a module expands to reveal its sub-features, and a breadcrumb shows where you are. Each sub-feature is its own focused screen; lists open a record in a side drawer. Below, each module gives its sub-features and what they do, the records it opens, and — in the tinted box — the workflows it takes part in, keyed to the SOP guide (B1, B4, and so on).

Finance & Accounting

One chart of accounts across the business, Kenya and Uganda held separately and consolidated on demand. It runs the full ledger and the day-to-day money workflows; every figure it produces reads the same everywhere else in Jikoni.
Sub-features
Sub-feature
What it does
Overview
Position at a glance — cash, payables, receivables, runway, approvals — with an income-statement snapshot and expense/revenue charts.
General Ledger
Chart of accounts, journal entries, trial balance and period close.
Payables
Supplier invoices captured against POs, three-way match and payables aging.
Receivables
eTIMS sales invoices, receipts/collection, debtor aging and dunning.
Bank & Cash
Bank and mobile-money accounts, reconciliation, and a cash forecast.
Petty Cash
Floats by custodian, coded receipt-backed vouchers, reconciliation and replenishment.
Budgets & Costing
Budget vs actual by cost centre, and project accounting on shared project codes.
Reporting & Compliance
P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance; VAT/eTIMS, withholding, Housing Levy; multi-currency; IFRS-for-SMEs.

WORKFLOWS  B1 Procure-to-Pay (payables, three-way match, GL posting) · B3 Order-to-Cash (receivables) · B4 Payroll → GL (the journal lands here) · B7 Inventory (asset depreciation posts here). See the Workflows & SOPs guide.

Procurement

Procure-to-pay on one connected chain: each document authorises the next, so nothing is ordered without an approved requisition and nothing is paid without a matched goods-received note.
Sub-features
Sub-feature
What it does
Overview
Spend by category and trend, the procure-to-pay chain, and exceptions holding payment.
Vendors
Registry with tax-compliance status, sanctions screening and performance ratings.
Requisitions
Purchase requisitions with live budget check and approval routing.
Purchase Orders
PO lifecycle — open, partially received, closed — raised only from an approved requisition.
Goods Received
Goods-received / delivery notes against a PO, with partial deliveries and photo evidence.
Sourcing / RFQ
Quote comparison with scoring and a documented award recommendation.
Contracts
Framework agreements at agreed rates, linked to the contract registry.
Records
A vendor — or any of its contracts — opens the vendor record: relationship history, contracts and every document shared (certificates, the signed contract, and the linked POs, GRNs and invoices).
Ignis-specific
Sanctions screening is a hard gate before award, per Procurement Manual IGN-PROC-001. Grant-funded buys must also satisfy the donor's own procurement rules.

WORKFLOWS  B1 Procure-to-Pay · B2 Raise a Requisition · B8 Vendor Onboarding & Sanctions Screening. See the Workflows & SOPs guide.

Sub-features
Sub-feature
What it does

Item catalogue with on-hand quantity, reorder point and valuation.

Stock across the central store and the county hubs.

Receipts, issues, transfers and adjustments — the movement ledger.

Human Resources

Staff files, leave, payroll, recruitment and the field workforce in one module, with statutory IDs and documents versioned and access-controlled on every record.
Sub-features
Sub-feature
What it does

Headcount, the HR to-do list, and workforce-composition charts.

Employee registry with contracts, statutory IDs (KRA PIN, NSSF, SHIF) and versioned documents.

Who can see and do what, granted per person and per module by a super admin — and the append-only record of everything done. This module underpins the controls in every other one.
Sub-features
Sub-feature

WORKFLOWS  B9 Access Provisioning; underpins the segregation-of-duties control in B1 and the audit trail everywhere. See the Workflows & SOPs guide.

Adjacent modules
Two further modules exist in the system and are being built out to the same depth; they are noted here for completeness:
Deployment & Carbon — deployment tracking and carbon / dMRV — where field installs and monitoring data feed carbon crediting. Draws on Inventory dispatch and Projects field activity.
Institution Readiness — the institutional readiness scoring that feeds the downstream CRM pipeline and, potentially, CleanCookIQ.
